BiSFit for World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.1)
Open in game with /bisfit or /sfit.
Gear data and combat estimates use WoWSims Forever: https://github.com/wowsims/forever
This addon has no affiliation with WoWSims or Blizzard.
The item catalog is a curated pre-raid subset. Recommendations rank tested sets in this snapshot.
The DPS profile is a reference Tauren, not the equipment, talents or buffs of the logged-in player.
For Restoration, the displayed casting HPS is a rank 6 Lesser Healing Wave single-spell model.
